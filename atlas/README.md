# The Pi Atlas

Ten chapters written to be read, not skimmed: precise enough to cite, plain
enough to enjoy. Every factual claim in them was checked against primary
sources or computed directly against this repository's
[verified digits](../data/README.md). Read in order for the full story, or
jump to whatever pulls at you.

| Chapter | What you will find |
|---|---|
| [01 · What is pi](01-what-is-pi.md) | The ratio itself: why it is the same for every circle, what irrational and transcendental actually mean, and how many digits anyone truly needs |
| [02 · Four thousand years of history](02-history.md) | The chase from Babylonian clay to 314 trillion digits, told through the people who pushed the number forward |
| [03 · The formula collection](03-formulas.md) | A scannable catalog of the great formulas, from Viete's nested roots to Chudnovsky, each with its story |
| [04 · How pi gets computed](04-algorithms.md) | Method, not history: what makes an algorithm fast, and why every record uses the same series |
| [05 · The digits themselves](05-digits.md) | The million decimals in this repository under a statistical microscope, landmarks included |
| [06 · Records](06-records.md) | Two ladders: machines grinding out trillions, and minds reciting tens of thousands |
| [07 · What nobody knows](07-open-problems.md) | Normality, irrationality measure, and the open questions that keep pi interesting |
| [08 · Pi in the physical world](08-pi-in-science.md) | Why a circle constant runs statistics, radio, and quantum mechanics: symmetry and bell curves |
| [09 · Pi in culture](09-culture.md) | The holiday, the poems, the song, the film, and one confused piece of 1897 legislation |
| [10 · Further reading](10-reading.md) | The books, papers, and sites worth your hours, each labeled for who it fits |

When reading turns into wanting to do: the [algorithms](../algorithms/README.md)
